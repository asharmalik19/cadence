How Cadence fits together

You state a goal and a deadline. An AI asks what it needs, gives a verdict on the deadline, and builds a system: weekly hours per kind of work, dated milestones, and daily tasks with a done criterion. When you miss tasks or something changes, it re-plans and says what moved.

One idea runs through all of it: the AI proposes, the backend checks, the database remembers.

The main pieces

The screens

frontend/src/

React. Shows what the backend sends and passes on your clicks. It decides nothing itself.

The rules

backend/app/services.py

FastAPI routes call these. Goal cap, what counts as missed, when to re-plan, all in one place.

The planner

backend/app/planner/

Writes the prompt and calls Gemini. fake.py gives canned plans for tests and free clicking.

The checks

backend/app/validation.py

Every AI reply is checked by code before it is saved: hours per day and week, a free day, deadlines.

The memory

backend/app/db.py

SQLite in data/cadence.db: settings, goals, tasks, updates.

One action, end to end: Next day

You leave a task unticked, click Next day and confirm the review. A moment later the goal shows a Last change note. Here is the trip that click takes.

Reference route: the stops below were not toured step by step in the editor. Open them with your editor's search.

  1. The click: frontend/src/components/ReviewDialog.tsx

    Search for const confirm. It gathers your ticks and updates and calls onConfirm. In frontend/src/App.tsx that calls nextDay from frontend/src/api.ts, which sends POST /api/day/next.

  2. The rules: backend/app/services.py

    Search for def next_day. It marks unticked tasks as missed, writes down why each goal needs a re-plan (missed tasks, a new update, or days away for Fast forward), and makes one AI call through plan_checked. Nothing to re-plan means no AI call.

  3. The check and the save: validation.py → db.py

    plan_checked (same file, near the top) asks the planner, runs check_plan in backend/app/validation.py, retries once with the problems listed, then gives up with an error. Only a reply that passes is saved, all in one db.transaction, so a failed AI call changes nothing. The new change_note becomes the Last change note on the goal page.

Where the proof lives

backend/tests/test_fast_forward.py runs this path with the fake planner. For example, test_ai_failure_leaves_day_ticks_and_updates_unchanged shows a failed re-plan leaves the day, ticks and updates as they were. Run all tests with uv run pytest.

A practice to reuse

Write the rule you care about as code, and don't trust the AI to follow it from the prompt alone.

In slice 4, the prompt said all goals must fit your time. In the first live three-goal run, the AI's plans added up to 50 h a week against a 36 h budget, and nothing noticed. The fix was a check in code, _check_weekly in backend/app/validation.py, plus a test that proves an overflowing plan is never saved: test_a_new_goal_that_overflows_the_week_is_not_saved in backend/tests/test_goal_cap_and_shared_time.py.

On any project that uses an AI, decide which promises must always hold (a budget, a format, a limit), check them in code after every AI reply, and retry or refuse when they fail. The prompt tells the AI what you want; the check makes sure you get it.
